Billing lives in **account settings -> billing**. Open it from the avatar menu in the dashboard header. That tab is where you see your current state, the machines you are being billed for, an estimated monthly total, and the buttons to choose a plan or manage payment.

<Callout type="info" title={"accounts from the beta"}>

Accounts that predate the billing system do not have a trial clock yet — the billing tab shows `free during beta — billing hasn't started` and nothing is restricted. They join the same 14-day trial described below on a single announced date, and owners get advance notice by email and a dashboard banner before that happens. Accounts created since then start their trial at signup.

</Callout>

## the free trial

Every account gets **14 days free**, at the pro feature level, with **no credit card**.

- Nothing is restricted during the trial. Roost, the public API, the CLI, the SDKs, and webhooks all work exactly as they do on a paid pro plan, so you evaluate the whole product before picking a tier.
- Trial state is tracked by owlette, not by Stripe. No subscription exists until you complete checkout, so there is nothing to cancel if you decide owlette is not for you — the trial simply ends.
- A countdown banner appears on the dashboard in the **last 7 days** of the trial. You can dismiss it; it comes back a week later until you choose a plan.
- Reminder emails go to the account owner on **day 10** ("4 days left"), **day 13** ("ends tomorrow"), and again when the trial ends.

Only the account owner — the user who owns the sites — sees trial banners and receives the emails. Members of somebody else's site are never billed for it and are never shown its trial state.

## choosing a plan

Click **choose a plan** on the trial banner, in the billing tab, or on any pro-feature upgrade card. Pick a tier, continue to Stripe Checkout, and add a card there. Billing starts at checkout — there is no second trial on the Stripe side.

| | core | pro |
|---|---|---|
| **price** | $10 per machine, per month | $50 per machine, per month |
| **minimum** | none | billed for at least 3 machines |
| **sites** | one site | unlimited sites with multi-site rbac |
| **machines and members** | unlimited | unlimited |
| **monitoring, auto-recovery, remote control** | included | included |
| **software and file deployment** | included | included |
| **display layouts with watchdog auto-revert** | included | included |
| **cortex AI fleet assistant** | included, bring your own API key | included, bring your own API key |
| **email alerts** | included | included |
| **public REST API, CLI, SDKs, webhooks** | not included | included |
| **roost project sync** | not included | included, 1 TiB storage per site |
| **support** | email | priority |

Cortex needs your own model API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, or a compatible provider) on both tiers. There are no per-user fees on either tier — members are free.

Core's single site is enforced, not just advertised: once you own a site on a core subscription, creating another answers `403` with code [`tier_insufficient`](/docs/api/errors#tier_insufficient) and the message "core includes one site — upgrade to pro for unlimited sites". Trial accounts are not limited — the trial runs at the pro feature level, so you can lay out as many sites as you like before choosing a tier.

Need volume pricing or an enterprise agreement? Mail [hey@tridant.io](mailto:hey@tridant.io). The full tier comparison also lives on the [pricing section of the owlette home page](/#pricing).

Switching tiers after you subscribe is not a self-service flow in the dashboard yet — the plan picker is for accounts that do not already have an active subscription. Contact support to move between core and pro.

## the 3-machine pro minimum

Pro bills a minimum of three machines per site. Written out: your bill counts `max(3, active machines)`.

An example. You run **two** machines on a pro site:

- active machines: 2
- billed machines: 3, because the minimum applies
- monthly charge: 3 x $50 = **$150**

Add a third machine and nothing changes — you are already paying for three. Add a fourth and the bill becomes 4 x $50 = $200.

The minimum is a **billing calculation, not a limit**. Nothing stops a pro site from running one machine, or none. It just does not cost less than three. Core has no minimum: it bills exactly the machines you run.

The billing tab says so inline — a pro site with two active machines shows `2 active machines — billed at the 3-machine pro minimum`, so the number on the invoice is never a surprise.

## how machines are counted

A machine counts as **active** when its agent has checked in within the last **7 days**.

- The check is the agent's last heartbeat, not its online/offline dot. A machine that was shut down cleanly last night still has a fresh heartbeat and still counts; a machine that has been crated and dark for two weeks does not.
- Seven days is deliberately generous. A touring rig in transit, a player behind a router reboot, or a kiosk off for a holiday should not drop off your invoice and then reappear on it.
- The count is taken as a **daily snapshot** and sent to Stripe once a day. Stripe keeps the latest value for the period, so removing a machine is reflected on the next day's snapshot rather than being averaged over the month.
- Machines that never reported a readable heartbeat are not counted. When we cannot prove a machine exists, you are not billed for it.

Your bill for a site is billed machines x the tier price, where billed machines is the raw count on core and `max(3, count)` on pro. The account total is the sum across every site you own.

The billing tab's estimated monthly total is exactly that arithmetic. It is an estimate: proration, tax, and mid-period changes are settled by Stripe, and the invoice in the billing portal is what was actually charged.

## roost storage

Roost is a pro feature. Each **pro site includes 1 TiB** of project storage.

- Storage past the inclusion is **$0.05 per GB**, where GB means a binary gibibyte (1024 x 1024 x 1024 bytes) — the same unit the 1 TiB inclusion is measured in.
- Overage is measured per site, summed across all your pro sites, then rounded **down** to whole GB once — not per site, so nothing is lost or double-counted in the rounding. A total three megabytes past the combined inclusion owes nothing.
- Core sites never accrue storage overage. Roost is not available on core, so any bytes a core site still holds from an earlier pro period are not billed.

**Storage overage billing activates when storage metering ships.** Today the daily usage job reports zero overage for every account because roost storage measurement is not yet wired to the underlying object store. Until then, uploads that would push a site past its included 1 TiB are refused with a quota error rather than being billed as overage — delete old roost versions to free space.

## what happens when a trial ends

If the trial ends without a plan, the account becomes **read-only**. It is a pause, not a deletion.

| what | while locked out |
|---|---|
| **viewing the dashboard** — metrics, status, history, screenshots, live view | works |
| **starting, stopping, restarting, or killing processes** | paused |
| **restarting machines, changing display layouts, editing machine or process config** | paused |
| **deployments, roost publish, sync, and rollback** | paused |
| **public API, CLI, and SDK calls** | paused |
| **webhook delivery** | paused while expired, resumes on reactivation — events are not queued up and replayed, and your subscriptions are never auto-disabled for the gap |
| **removing a machine or triggering an uninstall** | works — shrinking your fleet is how you lower your bill, so it is never blocked |
| **managing members, renaming or deleting a site, revoking agent tokens** | works — security actions never depend on a subscription |
| **agents reporting in** — heartbeats, metrics, logs, crash events | keeps running, so nothing is missing when you come back |
| **machine-offline alert emails** | keep arriving for 30 days after expiry, then stop |
| **your data** | nothing is deleted for non-payment; normal retention applies |

The dashboard shows a persistent notice explaining the state, and the controls that the server will refuse stay visible rather than being silently greyed out. A canceled subscription lands in the same state as an expired trial, with the same guarantees.

Nothing here is a data-retention event. Metrics and logs continue to age out under the ordinary 400-day retention policy and nothing else is removed, no matter how long an account sits unpaid.

## reactivating

Choose a plan at any time — during the trial, the day it ends, or months later. There is no separate reactivation flow.

1. Open **account settings -> billing**, or click the CTA on the lockout notice.
2. Pick core or pro and continue to Stripe Checkout.
3. Once Stripe confirms the subscription, controls, deploys, and API access come back and site tiers are set to the plan you chose.

Alert emails that were switched off after the 30-day post-expiry window resume as soon as the account is active again — you do not have to wait for the next daily sweep. Webhook delivery resumes on the next event for the same reason: entitlement is resolved live, not cached.

## managing billing

The billing tab shows:

- **where you stand** — a trial countdown, a renewal date, or the reason access is paused
- **each site you own** — its tier, active machine count, billed machine count, and monthly projection
- **roost storage** per pro site, once usage data exists, with the overage projection when a site is over
- **estimated monthly total** for the account

**manage billing** opens the Stripe customer portal, where payment method, invoices and receipts, tax details, and cancellation live. Stripe is the record of what was actually charged.

Cancelling in the portal keeps your subscription running to the end of the period you have already paid for. After that the account moves to the same read-only state described above, and choosing a plan restores it.

## api, cli, and sdk access

The public REST API, the `@owlette/cli` command line, and the Node and Python SDKs are **pro features**, and they are enforced server-side — there is no client-side gate to work around.

**While trialing.** Successful API-key responses carry an advisory header:

```http
X-Owlette-Billing-Warning: trial ends 2026-08-15T00:00:00.000Z; choose a plan to keep API access
```

Nothing is blocked while the header is present. The CLI prints it to stderr once per invocation (JSON output on stdout is untouched); the Node SDK surfaces it through the `onBillingWarning` client option and the Python SDK through `on_billing_warning`. Dashboard requests never carry it — the dashboard shows a banner instead.

**Once the trial ends.** Requests answer `402` with code [`trial_expired`](/docs/api/errors#trial_expired). The body carries the billing state so your own tooling can word its prompt.

**On a core subscription.** Pro-only operations — roost and chunk endpoints, creating a webhook, creating an API key — answer `403` with code [`tier_insufficient`](/docs/api/errors#tier_insufficient). Reading and revoking what you already have stays available, so a downgraded account can still audit and clean up its keys and webhook subscriptions.

Neither code should be retried until the underlying state changes. The full catalog, including the response envelope, is in [api errors](/docs/api/errors).

## faq

**Is there a free trial, and do I need a card?**
Yes — 14 days at the pro feature level, no card. A card is only collected at Stripe Checkout, when you choose a plan.

**What does it cost?**
Core is $10 per machine per month: monitoring, control, deploy, displays, and cortex on a single site. Pro is $50 per machine per month with a 3-machine minimum, and adds the public API, CLI, SDKs, webhooks, unlimited sites, and roost with 1 TiB of included project storage per site. No per-user fees on either.

**Are members or extra users charged?**
No. Both tiers include unlimited members. You pay per machine.

**Do I pay for machines that are switched off?**
Only if they checked in within the last 7 days. A machine dark for longer than that drops off the count until it reports again.

**I only run two machines. Do I really pay for three on pro?**
Yes — $150 a month is the floor for a pro site. If that does not suit a small fleet, core has no minimum, or mail [hey@tridant.io](mailto:hey@tridant.io) to talk about it.

**What happens if I never choose a plan?**
The dashboard becomes read-only: you can still see everything, agents keep reporting, and offline alerts keep arriving for another 30 days. Control actions, deploys, and API access pause. Nothing is deleted.

**Is my data deleted if I stop paying?**
No. Non-payment never deletes data. The ordinary 400-day retention policy is the only thing that removes anything.

**How is roost storage overage calculated?**
Bytes past the 1 TiB included per pro site, summed across your pro sites, rounded down to whole binary GB, at $0.05 per GB. Overage billing activates when storage metering ships.

**Does cortex cost extra?**
Cortex is included on both tiers, but it calls your own model provider with your own API key, so that provider bills you directly.

**Can I get an invoice or a receipt?**
Yes — every invoice, receipt, and payment method lives in the Stripe portal behind **manage billing**.
